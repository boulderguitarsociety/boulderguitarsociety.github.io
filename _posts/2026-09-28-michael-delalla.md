ABOUT MICHAEL DELALLA
> "One of the very best acoustic guitarists in the world....music of the most exquisite order." Matt Fink, All Music Guide

> Throughout a career spanning decades, Michael DeLalla’s fingerstyle guitar work is informed by his classical training and his love of jazz, Celtic and more far-reaching world traditions. His fluency on classical, 12-string, and steel-string acoustic guitar showcases gifted improvisation and dazzling technical facility that never sacrifices the richness and poignancy of his compositions. His extensive discography and concert appearances have drawn praise both at home and abroad. Also a passionate educator, he conducts workshops throughout the US based on his book 
[The Mindful Guitarist](https://www.fallingmountain.com/product/the-mindful-guitarist) (2015), where he explores his principles on the Creative Process for musical enthusiasts of all ages.

> "... a sublime and uniquely crafted sonic landscape…DeLalla creates a sonic vibrancy that is rich, remarkable and revelatory." Minor 7th.com, about his compositions on his recording _This Is How I Disappear_

In France, <ins>_terroir_</ins> refers to all the local environmental factors
that influence the uniqueness of a final product, typically wine.
Michael uses the term to reflect on all the factors that
influence his composing that lead to the unique qualities of
each indivdiual piece.  In tonight's program, Michael doesn't simply
play these pieces; he tells the stories that explain how they came to be,
so that we might understand and appreciate their _terroir_.

1. <ins>_Improv_</ins> dedicated to Roland Dyens (who always opened with an improv)
1. [_Arise_](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PfyEexqqm5k&t=3s)
1. [_Le Marchais du Malaucene_](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SJ6NjHVEGQg&t=18s) from witnessing the Tour de France
1. <ins>_Chief Niwot's Curse_</ins> (miniature no. 1) a study in all possible left hand slurs
1. <ins>_A Quiet Night on the Roaring Fork_</ins> (miniature no. 2) in memory of Jane Erb
1. <ins>_Her Secret Crush on Vinicius_</ins> (miniature no. 3), for Elaine and Vinicius Cantuária
1. [_Nana_](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q7W5sp2_-Y4&t=6s) (miniature no. 4) dedicated to percussionist Nana Vasconcelos
1. [_Waltz for Chat-colat_](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PX0BAgeEeAQ) for his cat
1. [_Nocturnal Scenes 'Round About Midnight_](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VbQu1ASPit8) an homage to Britten and Monk

The four miniatures will soon be published on [Guitar Chamber Press](https://www.guitarchambermusicpress.com/).
