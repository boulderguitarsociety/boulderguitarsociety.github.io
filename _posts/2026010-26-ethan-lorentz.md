Colorado-based guitarist [Ethan Lorentz](https://ethanlorentz.com/home) is a performer, composer, music educator, and scholar who has received acclaim as both a soloist, and a chamber musician. Ethan has a passion for expanding the guitar’s possibilities through performing new works and creating exciting new arrangements. These interests are evident on both of Ethan’s albums, [“Gallery”](https://lorentzlopezduo.bandcamp.com/album/gallery) and [“Schubert Lieder on Guitar”](https://www.discogs.com/release/26992809-Schubert-Ethan-Lorentz-Schubert-Lieder-On-Guitar-). The former is an album of guitar/flute duos inspired by 20th and 21st century artworks (featuring multiple new commissions) performed by the Lorentz/Lopez duo, and the latter project features a new set of Schubert arrangements for solo guitar. Ethan performed these arrangements in a lecture recital for the <ins>Guitar Foundation of America</ins>’s annual convention, where he has also lectured on the music of Leo Brouwer for the <ins>Symposium of Guitar Scholars</ins>. Ethan regularly performs as a soloist, with the Lorentz/Lopez duo, and has performed with several other ensembles, including the <ins>Aspen Music Festival Orchestra</ins>, the <ins>Boulder Chamber Choral</ins>, and the <ins>Nebula Ensemble</ins>.

The program featured works by Dowland, Bach, Schubert, Scarlatti, Ponce, and Ethan's own original compositions and arrangements on both 6 and 8 string guitars:
1. <ins>_Fantasia No. 7_</ins> by John Dowland
1. <ins>_Untitled_</ins> piece by Ethan Lorentz
1. <ins>_Untitled_</ins> 8-string guitar piece by Ethan Lorentz
1. <ins>_Prelude from BWV 998_</ins> by J.S. Bach Arr. For 8-string guitar Ethan Lorentz
1. <ins>_Der Lindenbaum_</ins> by Franz Schubert Arr. For 8-string guitar Ethan Lorentz
1. <ins>_Wasserflut_</ins> by Franz Schubert Arr. Ethan Lorentz
1. <ins>_Auf dem Flusse_</ins> by Franz Schubert Arr. Ethan Lorentz
1. <ins>_Sonata Kp. 208_</ins> by Domenico Scarlatti Arr. Richard Wright
1. <ins>_Sonata Kp. 322_</ins> by Domenico Scarlatti Arr. Richard Wright
1. <ins>_Theme varie et Finale_</ins> by Manuel Ponce
