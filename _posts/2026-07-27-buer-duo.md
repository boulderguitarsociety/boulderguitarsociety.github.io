![The Buer Duo](/pics/20260727-BuerDuo.png)

[Adam Buer](https://www.adambuer.com/) is a long-time friend of BGS with numerous performances
as one of our [featured artists](/Stars.html#Adam_Buer).  This June he played with the
<ins>GFA Community Guitar Orchestra</ins> on the final day of their conference here in Denver.
For more, see his [full bio](https://www.adambuer.com/about).

[Karin Buer](https://www.karinbuer.com/) teaches music theory and aural skills at CU,
as well as private piano lessons.  Read more about her research interests in her
[bio](https://www.karinbuer.com/about).  Tonight she plays violin and piano
to make it "The Buer Duo".

The theme for this performance is <ins>Musical Mariages: "Birds of a feather flock together"
and "opposites attract"</ins>, which you might think is about them but it says a lot about the amazing mash-ups below.
